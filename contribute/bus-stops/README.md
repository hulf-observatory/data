# Bus stop contributions: community data

Sent by residents through the Hyderabad Urban Observatory's form at
https://hyderabad.urbanobservatory.in/contribute/bus-stops/ . Updated daily.

- `bus-stops-reports.csv` and `bus-stops-reports.geojson` hold the same rows.
- `bus-stops-summary.csv` and `bus-stops-summary.geojson` (from 18 Nov 2026): one row per place, combining its rows: the
  current answer to each question, whether people agree on it, and how many gave each answer (see below).
- Times are UTC (ISO 8601). Coordinates are WGS 84. `ward` and `zone` are the 2026 corporation wards, worked out
  from the coordinates.
- `ramp` (is there a working ramp?) yes / no / dk replaced the `access` tick-box (ramp, tactile paving, none) on 17 Nov 2026; `access` stays for the answers sent before.
- `dk` = the person said they don't know (offered on every one-answer question from 17 Nov 2026, instead of skipping it). It is not a real answer: it has no score, does not count towards the 3 answers needed, and is not counted as an answer in a stop's summary (counted separately as `<question>_dk`).
- `prefilled` = true: the stop already had answers, so the form started from its answer to each question (until 18 Nov 2026 the latest answer, since then the current answer from the summary) and the person changed only what was different (from 17 Nov 2026). A person who changed nothing sends a `confirmed` check instead of a row.
- Published times (`observed_at` on the map and in the download) are rounded down to the hour, since rows carry a nickname (from 16 Nov 2026).
- One row per contribution about a bus stop. A stop can have many; the summary file combines them.
- `stop_id` is the stop's id in TGSRTC's open GTFS data (June 2026, Open Data Telangana). Stops residents added because they were missing from that data have ids starting `new-`, `place_type` = `missing`, and the coordinates of the pin they placed. For other stops the coordinates are TGSRTC's.
- `place_type`: how the stop was picked: `gps` (from the nearest stops to the phone; the phone's location is not kept), `search` (by name or area, or tapped on a map), `preset` (from the stop's page), `missing` (added on a map; since 16 Nov 2026 within 100 m of where the person stood), `map` (since 16 Nov 2026: the phone's location failed, so the person found the stop under a pin on a map, at the stop). `located`: phone or map, how the person's own place was found.
- `stop_place` (asked only of people at the stop who shared their location): is the stop where TGSRTC's data puts it? yes / here (no, it is where the person stood: `stop_lat`, `stop_lng` are their phone's location) / marked (no, they dragged it: `stop_lat`, `stop_lng` are where they put it) / dk (not sure). Corrections are as given, not checked.
- `here`: `yes` if the person said they were at the stop, `no` if they described a stop they use from memory.
- Answers (empty = skipped, or not asked because of an earlier answer): `shelter` yes / no; if yes `shelter_state` good / broken and `shelter_protects` (from sun and rain) yes / partly / no; if no `shade` (from trees or buildings) yes / partly / no. `seats` yes / no; if yes `seats_state` good (comfortable) / poor (uncomfortable) / broken. `footpath` yes / no; if yes `blocked` no / partly / badly and `pass` (can walk or wheel past without stepping onto the road) yes / no. `access` ramp (working), tactile (paving), or none. `crossing` (a pedestrian crossing near the stop: zebra, signal, foot overbridge or subway) yes / no; if yes `crossing_safe` (feels safe to cross) yes / somewhat / no. `bus_stops_at` kerb / step (one step out) / lane (in the traffic lane) / away (away from the stop). `view` ads or structures block the view of buses or stop information. `room`: enough room to wait at busy times, yes / no. `light` (at night) yes / no / dk (don't know); if yes `light_state` good / poor. `safe_day` and `safe_night` (how safe it feels to wait there during the day / after dark) safe / somewhat / unsafe. `floods` (water-logs when it rains) yes / no / dk (don't know). `name_sign` yes / no; if yes `name_state` clear (easy to read) / faded. `route_info` yes / no; if yes `route_state` current / outdated. `facilities` dustbin, water (drinking), toilet (in sight), cctv, board (live arrival), or none. Several values are separated by `;`.
- `improve`: one thing the person would improve, in their words. `nick`: the nickname they play under on the leaderboard, if any (made up; not a real name; its PIN is never published).
- `confirmed`: how many people later said the stop is still like this; `disputed`: how many said something is wrong (asked until 18 Nov 2026; since then people who disagree send their own answers instead).
- `located`: how the place was given — `phone` (the phone's location, standing there), `map` (standing there, but the phone's location failed, so the person put the place under a pin on a map) or `memory` (not there when sending: the person knows the place well and marked it on the map from memory; `observed_at` is then the time it was sent). Use it to weigh rows: `phone` is the most reliable. `here`: `yes` (at the stop) or `known` (from memory).
- `outvoted` (from 18 Nov 2026): how many of this row's answers differ from the stop's agreed answer (see the summary file). Only questions with an agreed answer count, and only answers recent enough to count for that question. A high number means the row disagrees with most people; it is not hidden for that.
- `bus-stops-summary.csv` and `bus-stops-summary.geojson` (from 18 Nov 2026): one row per stop, built from all its visible rows. `key` (the stop id), `name` (stop and direction), `lat`, `lng` (the stop), `n` (rows), `latest` (date of the newest row), `checks` ("still the same" checks). For each question: `<question>` the current answer (blank if no one answered recently, or answers are mixed; several values separated by `|`), `<question>_agreed` yes (at least 2 people answered recently and more than two-thirds gave this answer) / no (only 1 recent answer) / mixed (2 or more answers without a two-thirds majority) / blank, `<question>_counts` how many people gave each answer (for example `yes:3|no:1`; for tick-all questions, how many ticked each option), `<question>_dk` how many said they don't know (not counted as an answer). Follow-up questions count only answers where the question above matched, and are blank unless the current answer above asks for them. For tick-all questions an option is current when more than two-thirds of the answers ticked it.
- Answers stop counting after a while, by question (`keep_days`): about 3 years for things that rarely change (`shelter`, `shade`, `seats`, `footpath`, `ramp`, `crossing`, `name_sign`, `route_info`, `light`), 6 months for things that change fast (`blocked`, `pass`, `light_state`), a year for the rest. Older answers stay in the rows file.
- Points on the leaderboard (from 18 Nov 2026): 10 for each row at the stop (the first at a stop or an update), 5 for a row from memory, 15 more for adding a missing stop, 5 more for a photo, 2 for a check or flag. Rows removed as spam earn nothing.
- The questions are a shortened version of a 2026 bus stop audit form used in Bengaluru.
- Not checked on the ground by us. `confirmed`: how many other people (one count per device) later said the row is
  still right; `disputed`: how many said something about it is wrong. Rows flagged as spam are left out; photos are
  not included.
- No names, phone numbers or IP addresses are collected.
